# Staff guide

## The one rule

**Every inquiry goes into the system, from every hotline and every social channel, as soon as
you reply to it.** If it's not in the system, nobody will follow up.

## Coordinator: logging an inquiry (about 20 seconds)

1. Click **+ New inquiry**.
2. **Contact number**: type it any way (`0771234567`, `+94 77 123 4567`). The system shows
   whether this person is a **new customer** or an **existing customer** with open inquiries.
   - If they already have an open inquiry for the same thing, open it and log a follow-up
     instead of creating a new one.
3. **Product of interest**: start typing; pick from the suggestions when possible.
4. **When do they want to buy?** Today / This week / This month / Just checking.
   Ask: *"When are you planning to buy?"*
5. **Source**: which hotline or social channel the message came from. The system remembers
   your last choice.
6. **Assign to** a salesperson (or leave unassigned and assign later).
7. Optional: name, payment type (cash, card, installment, trade-in), budget, notes.
8. Tick **agreed to receive offers** only if the customer said yes.
9. Press **Save & log another** when you have more to log from the same hotline.

**Social media tip:** Facebook, Instagram and TikTok messages often have no phone number.
Ask early: *"Can I have your WhatsApp number so our team can send you the price and stock
details?"*

## Salesperson: working your list

Open **Follow-ups** (or the Dashboard) at the start of the day and work **top to bottom**:

1. **Overdue** (red): these should have been contacted already.
2. **Due later today**: sorted by urgency (Today buyers first).
3. **Needs decision**: all automatic follow-ups are used up. Mark sold or lost, or set a date.

For each customer:

1. Tap **WhatsApp** (opens a chat with a ready message) or **Call**.
2. Tap **Log result** and choose what happened:
   - *Reached, talked*, *Price / quote sent*, *Will visit the shop*, *Asked to contact later*,
     *No answer / no reply*, *Bought (sold)*, *Not buying (lost)* plus the reason.
3. Add a short note (price given, colour, trade-in phone...).
4. Leave **Next follow-up** empty to use the automatic schedule, or set a date if the
   customer asked ("call me after the 25th").

When you log a result on an unassigned lead, it becomes yours.

## Automatic follow-up schedule

| Customer said | First follow-up | After that |
|---|---|---|
| Today | in 2 hours | next day, then +2 and +4 days |
| This week | next morning 10:00 | +2, +4, +7 days |
| This month | in 3 days | +7, +7, +14 days |
| Just checking | in 7 days | +14, +30 days |

## Daily routine

| Time | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00 | Sales | Clear **Overdue** and **Due today** |
| 13:00 | Coordinator | Check every hotline and social inbox; anything not logged gets logged |
| 13:00 | Coordinator | Assign all **unassigned** leads (Dashboard tile) |
| 18:00 | Coordinator | Second inbox sweep; return missed calls |
| 18:30 | Manager | Dashboard: overdue count per person should be close to zero |
| Weekly | Manager | **Reports**: which sources sell, lost reasons, response time |

## Good habits

- Always record a **lost reason**. That's how we learn whether we lose on price, stock or speed.
- Reply fast: the Reports page shows the **median time to first response** and the
  percentage answered within 15 minutes.
- Never send promotions to customers who did not agree to receive offers.
